> Now you can write with your favorite AI beside you, or on your own, and enjoy every line of it.

## Why I made it

I love to write. Articles, notes, stories from my own life, anything really. I'm someone who likes a lot of things, tries a lot of things, and enjoys telling stories about all of them. Somewhere along the way I noticed that whatever gets told, and whatever gets read aloud, it all starts as writing.

We can write things down in our memory. But words in a file, or on a canvas, are what make them last.

Look around. The video you watched started as a script. The song started as lyrics. The app you're using started as code, and code is writing too. As someone who writes code myself, most of what I make begins as words on a screen. A lot of big things in this world were born from a piece of writing. So write, and let your writing give something back to the world.

## Why it looks like this

I also care a lot about how a product feels and how it flows. typeSirkel is my answer to that. Your writing stays a plain file in your own folder, the editor stays quiet, and the AI comes when you call it, not the other way round.

## I could use your help

typeSirkel is still young, and I don't know everything writers out there need. If something gets in your way, feels missing, or you wish it were there, tell me on [GitHub](https://github.com/aconymouse/typesirkel/issues) or at [aconymous.com/chat](https://aconymous.com/chat). Code, translations, prompts, even a small bug report, it all counts.

One small step at a time, let's make a change together.
