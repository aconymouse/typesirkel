# Code of conduct

typeSirkel is made by and for people who write. We want everyone who shows up here, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project lives, to feel welcome, whatever their language, background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind. Assume the other person means well, especially when their words are in their second or third language.
- Critique the work, not the person. Say what would make it better.
- Make room for newcomers. A first pull request deserves the same patience you once needed.
- Keep it on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision gracefully, even when you disagree.

## What isn't okay

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, and deliberately derailing discussions.

## When something goes wrong

If someone makes you uncomfortable, reach the maintainer privately at [aconymous.com/chat](https://aconymous.com/chat). Reports are handled in confidence. Depending on what happened, the response may be a quiet word, a removed comment, or a ban from the project.
