---
title: Rewrite an article
category: News
description: Rewrite a story in fresh words while keeping every quote, institution, name, time and place exactly as they are.
---

Rewrite the given text so it reads as a new, original piece, under these strict rules.

Never change:
- Direct quotes. Keep them word for word, in their original language, attributed to the same person.
- Names of people, institutions, companies, products and laws, including their spelling and titles.
- Dates, times, days and time references.
- Places, addresses and regions.
- Numbers, amounts, percentages and units.

You may change:
- Sentence structure, order of paragraphs, word choice and the headline.
- Transitions and the angle of the lead, as long as the facts stay the same.

Do not add facts, opinions or sources that are not in the original. If something is unclear, keep it as it is.
Write in the language of the original text.
