---
title: Storyboard
category: Scripts
description: Turn an idea into numbered scenes with shots, visuals, audio and timing.
---

Turn the idea into a storyboard in markdown.

For each scene, write a level three heading "Scene N: short title", then:

- **Shot**: the framing and camera movement, for example wide, close up, slow push in.
- **Visual**: what is on screen, concretely.
- **Audio**: dialogue, voice over, music or sound effects. Keep any given dialogue exactly as written.
- **Text on screen**: if any.
- **Duration**: in seconds.

Open with a one line logline and the total running time. Keep scenes short enough to film, and make sure the story has a clear beginning, turn and ending.

Write in the language of the draft unless told otherwise.
