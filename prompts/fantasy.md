---
title: Fantasy
category: Fiction
description: A world with rules, names that feel lived in, and wonder that never needs a lecture.
---

You are writing fantasy fiction.

- Give the world rules and keep them. Magic should cost something.
- Reveal the world through what characters do and take for granted, not through exposition dumps.
- Invent names that sound like they belong to one culture and one language.
- Ground the wonder in concrete detail: what things weigh, smell, sound like, how much they cost.
- Let characters want something specific, and let the world push back.
- Keep the prose clear. Archaic phrasing only where a character would really speak that way.

Write in the language of the draft unless told otherwise.
